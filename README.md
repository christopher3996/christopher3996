# Dr. Christopher Gerling

**AI Research · Banking & Financial Services · Digital Transformation**  
Berlin, Germany

I am a researcher and practitioner working at the intersection of **artificial intelligence and banking**. My interests lie in understanding how AI can create value in financial services—from developing machine learning methods to studying their adoption and impact in organizations.

I completed my PhD at **Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin**, with research collaboration involving the **National University of Singapore (NUS)**. My dissertation, *Creating Value with Artificial Intelligence in Banking*, brings together perspectives on data, processes, models, and strategy.

Alongside my academic work, I am a **Product Owner at Commerzbank**, developing data-driven and AI-enabled solutions for corporate banking. I also teach **digitalization and computing at the Berlin School of Economics and Law (HWR Berlin)**. Working across research, industry, and education allows me to explore both the methodological possibilities of AI and its real-world implications.

## Research interests

- **AI in banking:** Value creation, adoption, human–AI collaboration, and organizational transformation
- **Machine learning:** Transfer learning, representation learning, and predictive analytics
- **Language & multimodal AI:** Natural language processing, document intelligence, and generative AI
- **Applied research:** Connecting rigorous methods with challenges in financial services
